Kinbo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Kinbo (কিনবো) is a multi-vendor shopping app for Bangladesh, operated by Ai Shagor ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and your choices. বাংলাদেশি ব্যবহারকারীদের জন্য সারসংক্ষেপ নিচে দেওয়া আছে।

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and password (stored securely by Google Firebase Authentication — we never see your password).
  • Shop profile (only if you create a shop): shop name, your name, phone number, district, shop photo, and the payment account numbers you choose to save (bKash, Nagad, Rocket, bank, Payoneer, Binance or other). Payment details you save are shown to buyers of your products so they can pay you directly.
  • Products and orders: product listings you publish (title, price, photos, description) and order information (buyer name, phone, delivery address, items, amounts, affiliate code used).
  • Advertising: the app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect a device advertising identifier to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's own privacy policy.

How payments work

Kinbo does not collect, hold, or process payments. Buyers pay sellers directly using the payment details the seller publishes in the app (for example bKash), or cash on delivery. Affiliate commissions and platform fees are recorded in the app as amounts owed and are settled manually between the people involved. Never share your PIN, password, or OTP with anyone — Kinbo will never ask for them.

How information is used

  • To operate the marketplace: showing products, processing orders, and displaying seller payment details to buyers.
  • To calculate and display affiliate commissions and platform fees.
  • To keep accounts secure and to moderate listings (the admin may hide products or block abusive shops).

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Shop profile details and product listings are visible to other app users by design (that is how buyers find and pay you). Order details are visible to the buyer, the seller, the affiliate who earned on that order, and the app admin. Backend services are provided by Google Firebase; ads are provided by Google AdMob.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. To delete your account and associated data, email us at aishagormultimidea@gmail.com from your account email and we will delete your profile, products, and payment details. Some order records may be kept where needed for dispute resolution.

Children

Kinbo is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
